## 2. Legislative & Behavioral Landscape

### Single-Use Plastic Ban & TNPCB
- **The Mandate:** Tamil Nadu implemented a strict statewide ban on 14 categories of single-use plastic (SUP) products effective January 1, 2019.
- **Meendum Manjapai Campaign (Behavioral Trigger):** Launched in December 2021 by the state government to replace SUPs with traditional yellow cloth bags. This massive state-sponsored behavior change initiative indicates a highly receptive public and political will for eco-driven citizen action.

### The TASMAC DRS Catalyst (Crucial Trigger Event)
- **Context:** The Tamil Nadu State Marketing Corporation (TASMAC) holds a monopoly over wholesale and retail vending of alcoholic beverages in the state.
- **The Court Mandate:** Driven by environmental concerns and wildlife injuries in hill stations (like the Nilgiris) due to broken glass, the **Madras High Court** issued directives for a bottle return system to curb littering.
- **The Pilot (Current Action):** 
  - TASMAC is actively deploying automated, AI-enabled bottle deposit machines (e.g., at the Egmore outlet in Chennai).
  - Customers return bottles and receive a **₹10 refund instantly via a UPI QR scan**.
- **The MRP Shift (2026 Proposal):** 
  - TASMAC has proposed to the High Court that the ₹10 refundable deposit be baked directly into the Maximum Retail Price (MRP) of liquor bottles.
  - The responsibility for collection and recycling will shift to the liquor/beer manufacturers.
  - This mandate will require a **QR code-based tracking mechanism** to trace bottle lifecycles and process refunds.

### Perfect Product-Market Fit
Retearn is uniquely positioned to dominate the Tamil Nadu market due to the exact requirements of the Madras High Court and TASMAC's pilot.

- **The UPI / QR Mandate vs. reklaim Tech:**

- **Manufacturer Responsibility & Traceability:**
  - Manufacturers are being forced to track bottle lifecycles using QR codes.
  - *The Wedge:* Retearn's digital infrastructure provides end-to-end QR/CV tracking, offering manufacturers bulletproof audit trails to satisfy High Court scrutiny.
- **Computer Vision (CV) vs. Barcodes:**
  - TASMAC bottles are notoriously prone to label damage.
  - *The Wedge:* While generic RVMs (like Biocrux) rely on intact barcodes, Retearn's **reklaim Pro / Mini** utilizes Computer Vision to identify glass/PET even without a barcode, drastically increasing the uptime and acceptance rate in rough Indian conditions.
